# Nanna / Ningal

Sumerian moon is not Selene. Selene is Greek.

## Nanna / Suen / Sin

The moon itself. Sumerian **Nanna** (also **Suen**). Akkadian later says **Sin**.

- Son of Enlil and Ninlil.
- City god of Ur. Temple Ekišnugal.
- Sign: recumbent crescent. Animal: bull.
- Written as the number **30** — days in the month.

That last tick is the two-worlds move: the name is a number.

## Ningal

The moon *goddess* in this system. Sumerian **Nin-gal**, “Great Queen.” Akkadian **Nikkal**.

- Wife of Nanna/Suen.
- Lady / mother of Ur.
- Children include Utu (sun) and Inanna (Venus) — not the moon.

Ningal is the consort, not the disk. The disk is Nanna. Both belong to the same house.
