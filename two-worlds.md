# two-worlds

Imported from https://github.com/Azdwarf5Azdwarf/two-worlds

Spoken thought and typed thought are not the same world.

## The realization

People say I mumble.

What happens: I already have the sentence. When speech is too fast, the two worlds misalign. Consonants drop. Only vowels come out.

- To me it still feels like the full word.
- To others it is mostly vowels.

Swedish: *konsonanter* fall off. Slow down and they come back.

Typing is the other world. Sit down and write and the idea becomes a different, better shape than if it were spoken.

- Speech through geometry?
- Speech to words?

## Torah / abjad

The Torah is often written without vowels. Beautiful. Skeleton of consonants. The reader supplies the breath.

Flip it for speech:

- Written language: consonants. Pure content. What remains is the bone — like *father*.
- Spoken language: the vowel-less stream, then vowels replaced.

Like a Sumerian move: replace vowels with numbers. Frequencies.

Consonants = content.
Vowels = pitch / Hz / number.

Is language going to be pure content first?
