*Tidvatten* is already the compound. Time glued to water. The moon moves that word.

Nanna pulls the water. The clock is 60 because the moon-name is 30, doubled.

## Why 60 stuck

60 is the smallest integer divisible by 1–6 (also 10, 12, 15, 20, 30).
Clocks, degrees, minutes, seconds. Decimal day failed.
